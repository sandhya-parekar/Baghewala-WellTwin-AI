# Baghewala Heavy Oil Field Profile
## Bikaner-Nagaur Basin, Rajasthan, India
**Operator**: Oil India Limited (OIL)

---

### 1. Geological & Reservoir Characteristics
- **Basin**: Bikaner-Nagaur Basin (Western Rajasthan)
- **Primary Formations**:
  - **Jodhpur Sandstone**: Early Cambrian fluvial to shallow-marine sandstones with moderate permeability (50–300 mD).
  - **Bilara Dolomite/Carbonate**: Karstified dolomite with vuggy porosity and natural fracture network.
- **Reservoir Depth**: ~1,000 m to 1,120 m true vertical depth (TVD).
- **Native Reservoir Temperature**: 58°C – 64°C.
- **Initial Reservoir Pressure**: 92 bar – 105 bar (hydrostatically normal to slightly depleted).

---

### 2. Heavy Crude Physical & Chemical Properties
- **API Gravity**: 16° – 18.5° API (High density: ~0.945 – 0.960 g/cm³).
- **Dead Oil Dynamic Viscosity**:
  - At native reservoir temperature (60°C): **1,800 to 5,500+ cP**.
  - At heated steam stimulation conditions (120°C): **35 to 65 cP**.
  - At near-wellbore steam injection (200°C): **10 to 18 cP**.
- **Asphaltene Content**: 12.0% – 17.5% by weight (causes non-Newtonian shear-thinning and prone to flocculation).
- **Resin Content**: High (stabilizes heavy polar hydrocarbons).

---

### 3. Thermal Recovery: Cyclic Steam Stimulation (CSS)
CSS, commonly known as **Huff-and-Puff**, operates in discrete cyclic phases:
1. **Steam Injection Phase**: 2,000 to 3,200 tons of 80% quality steam injected at 85–105 bar and 280°C–310°C over 14–21 days.
2. **Soak Phase**: Shut-in for 4–7 days to permit conductive thermal equilibrium into cold matrix.
3. **Thermal Production Phase**: Sucker rod pump lifts hot, mobile crude at peak rates (100–180 BPD).
4. **Cooling & Viscosity Decline**: Over 60–120 days, heat dissipates into overburden/underburden. Viscosity rises, sandface inflow decreases.
5. **Economic Cut-off**: Production terminates when temp approaches ~65°C–68°C to avert severe rod drag.

---

### 4. Artificial Lift: Sucker Rod Pump (SRP) Challenges in Heavy Oil
1. **Rod Floating**: On downstroke, buoyant weight of steel rod string is counteracted by viscous skin friction. Plunger descent lags walking beam horsehead motion, causing polished rod clamp separation and sudden impact.
2. **Compressive Fatigue**: Lower rod string buckles helically, causing coupling abrasion against production tubing.
3. **High Peak Polished Rod Load (PPRL)**: Upstroke must lift both fluid hydrostatic column and viscous boundary layer.
4. **Volumetric Efficiency Slip**: Viscous delay in traveling valve ball seating causes backflow and gas locking.
