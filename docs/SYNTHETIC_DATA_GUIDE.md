## 1. How the Data is Generated

The dataset is generated via **deterministic first-principles physics and empirical heavy oil correlations** implemented in [`scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py`](file:///C:/Users/ADMIN/Desktop/Baghewala-WellTwin-AI/scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py).

The generation engine models the coupled behavior of 5 wells over a continuous **90-day timeline with hourly timesteps (2,160 hours/well = 10,800 total records)**:

```
Reservoir Heat & Pressure (CSS Huff & Puff) 
       │
       ▼
Walther Viscosity Model (T -> Viscosity)
       │
       ▼
Heavy Oil Inflow Performance (IPR)
       │
       ▼
Sucker Rod Pump Mechanics (API RP 11L: PPRL, MPRL, Fluid Drag)
       │
       ▼
Equipment Risk & Rod Float State Engine
```

### Core Physics & Simulated Relationships:

1. **Walther / Arrhenius Viscosity Thinning**:
   Heavy oil dynamic viscosity $\mu$ is exponentially coupled to reservoir temperature $T$:
   $$\mu(T) = \mu_{\text{ref}} \cdot \exp\left(B \cdot \left(\frac{1}{T_K} - \frac{1}{T_{\text{ref},K}}\right)\right)$$
   - At cold native reservoir temperature ($60^\circ\text{C}$): $\mu \approx 3,400 - 4,500\text{ cP}$
   - At stimulated heat ($88^\circ\text{C}$): $\mu \approx 900 - 1,200\text{ cP}$
   - At peak steam temperature ($115^\circ\text{C}$): $\mu \approx 150 - 250\text{ cP}$
   - In steam chamber ($140^\circ\text{C}$): $\mu \approx 35 - 55\text{ cP}$

2. **Inflow Performance Relationship (IPR)**:
   Inflow rate is heavily throttled by oil viscosity ($\propto \mu^{-0.44}$), driving deliverability from $>160\text{ BPD}$ when hot down to $<40\text{ BPD}$ when cold.

3. **CSS Thermal Cycle Progression**:
   Models the 4 operational phases:
   - `INJECTION`: High steam volume ($140 - 220\text{ tpd}$), high injection pressure ($88 - 98\text{ bar}$), reservoir temperature ramps up smoothly ($60^\circ\text{C} \to 145^\circ\text{C}$). Sucker rod pump is stopped ($SPM = 0$, $Q_o = 0$).
   - `SOAK`: Steam shut-in ($0\text{ tpd}$), latent heat diffuses conductively into cold rock, soak hours count up ($0 \to 144\text{ hrs}$). Pump remains stopped.
   - `PRODUCTION`: Sucker rod pump starts lifting hot crude ($SPM = 4.8 - 7.5$, $Q_o = 120 - 175\text{ BPD}$). Temperature follows gradual conductive cooling decay: $T(t) = T_{\text{native}} + \Delta T_{\text{peak}} \cdot e^{-\lambda t}$.
   - `COOLING`: Late-stage depletion when $T < 68^\circ\text{C}$ (cut-off threshold). Viscosity surges past $2,000\text{ cP}$, triggering the next steam stimulation cycle.

4. **Sucker Rod Pump (SRP) & Rod Floating Dynamics (API RP 11L)**:
   - **Peak Polished Rod Load (PPRL)**: Upstroke tension combining buoyant rod weight ($W_r$), fluid load ($F_o$), dynamic acceleration, and upward fluid drag. Overloads ($>20,000\text{ lbs}$) trigger `rod_failure_risk`.
   - **Minimum Polished Rod Load (MPRL)**: Downstroke tension: $MPRL = W_r - F_{\text{viscous}} - F_{\text{accel}}$.
   - **Rod Floating Hazard**: When heavy oil cools ($>1,800\text{ cP}$) or $SPM$ is excessive, downward viscous drag overcomes buoyant rod weight, causing $MPRL < 2,200\text{ lbs}$. Rods go slack, traveling valve hangs, and rods buckle against tubing!

5. **Diverse Baseline Well Personas**:
   - **`BGW-01`** *(Thermal Pilot)*: Mid-production phase of CSS Cycle 4. Balanced temperature ($88.5^\circ\text{C}$), viscosity ($1,100\text{ cP}$), healthy status ($88/100$).
   - **`BGW-02`** *(High Viscosity / Cooling Watch)*: Late cooling phase of Cycle 3. Reservoir cooled to $62.4^\circ\text{C}$ (below $68^\circ\text{C}$ cutoff), viscosity $2,250\text{ cP}$, $MPRL = 2,080\text{ lbs}$. **Critical Rod Floating Hazard**!
   - **`BGW-03`** *(North Extension)*: Early peak production of Cycle 5. Hot well ($114.5^\circ\text{C}$), low viscosity ($165\text{ cP}$), high production ($168.2\text{ BPD}$), optimal health.
   - **`BGW-04`** *(South Flank Appraisal)*: Day 4 of CSS Cycle 2 **Soak Phase**. Hot shut-in ($144.0^\circ\text{C}$), $SPM = 0$, $Q_o = 0$, soak time = $96.0\text{ hrs}$.
   - **`BGW-05`** *(Deep Sector)*: Cycle 4 production ($1110\text{m}$ depth). Inflow cannot fill pump barrel ($61.5\%$ fillage). **Fluid Pound & Impact Loading Risk** on dynacards!

## 2. Field Dictionary (25 Telemetry Attributes)

| Field Name | Type | Unit | Description & Physical Range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `well_id` | Text | — | Well asset identifier (`BGW-01` to `BGW-05`). |
| `timestamp` | Timestamp | — | Timestamp in `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS` format (hourly). |
| `reservoir_temperature_c` | Float | °C | Near-wellbore reservoir temperature ($58^\circ\text{C} - 152^\circ\text{C}$). |
| `reservoir_pressure_bar` | Float | bar | Average reservoir drainage pressure ($70 - 118\text{ bar}$). |
| `oil_viscosity_cp` | Float | cP | Dynamic crude viscosity calculated via Walther equation ($30 - 4,500\text{ cP}$). |
| `steam_volume_tpd` | Float | tpd | Steam injection rate ($140 - 220\text{ tpd}$ during injection, $0$ during production). |
| `injection_pressure_bar` | Float | bar | Wellhead steam injection pressure ($88 - 98\text{ bar}$). |
| `soak_time_hr` | Float | hrs | Cumulative shut-in soak duration ($0 - 144\text{ hrs}$). |
| `production_bpd` | Float | BPD | Gross surface oil rate ($0\text{ BPD}$ during soak/inj; $30 - 180\text{ BPD}$ during prod). |
| `production_cutoff_bpd` | Float | BPD | Economic low-rate abandonment threshold ($28 - 35\text{ BPD}$). |
| `stroke_length_in` | Float | in | Sucker rod surface unit stroke length ($120 - 168\text{ in}$). |
| `spm` | Float | SPM | Pumping speed in strokes per minute ($0\text{ SPM}$ idle, $4.5 - 7.5\text{ SPM}$ normal). |
| `vfd_speed_rpm` | Float | RPM | Prime mover motor RPM through gear reducer ($0 - 1,350\text{ RPM}$). |
| `rod_load_lbs` | Float | lbs | Peak Polished Rod Load on upstroke ($15,000 - 21,500\text{ lbs}$). |
| `pump_efficiency_pct` | Float | % | Volumetric pump fillage efficiency ($55\% - 95\%$). |
| `energy_consumption_kwh`| Float | kWh | Total hourly electrical energy consumption ($15 - 45\text{ kWh}$). |
| `css_cycle` | Integer | count | Current Cyclic Steam Stimulation cycle number ($2 - 5$). |
| `css_phase` | Text | — | Current operational phase: `INJECTION`, `SOAK`, `PRODUCTION`, `COOLING`. |
| `equipment_risk_score` | Float | 0–100 | Composite mechanical asset risk score ($0$ = safe, $100$ = severe danger). |
| `equipment_risk_level` | Text | — | Risk category: `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`. |
| `rod_floating_risk` | Float | 0.0–1.0 | Likelihood of downstroke rod string slack & compressive buckling. |
| `impact_loading_risk` | Float | 0.0–1.0 | Likelihood of hydraulic shock loads due to fluid pound or rod float snap. |
| `pump_unsetting_risk` | Float | 0.0–1.0 | Likelihood of pump anchor slippage under extreme upstroke pull. |
| `rod_failure_risk` | Float | 0.0–1.0 | Likelihood of tensile parted rod or thread fatigue failure. |
| `water_cut_pct` | Float | % | Produced water volume fraction ($38\% - 75\%$). |

## 3. How to Regenerate the Data

The simulation pipeline is completely repeatable and self-contained. Run from the project root:

```cmd
python scripts\generate_synthetic_data.py
```

### What Happens Automatically:
1. Re-initializes the SQLite database tables in [`database/welltwin.db`](file:///C:/Users/ADMIN/Desktop/Baghewala-WellTwin-AI/database/welltwin.db).
2. Generates 10,800 hourly records across the 5 Baghewala wells.
3. Computes daily aggregates (455 records in `production_history`).
4. Generates surface and downhole dynamometer cards (dynacards) in `srp_readings`.
5. Emits real-time diagnostic alarms in `alerts` and workover events in `maintenance_events`.
6. Exports all 4 CSV datasets to the [`data/`](file:///C:/Users/ADMIN/Desktop/Baghewala-WellTwin-AI/data/) folder.

## 6. How the Frontend Receives and Displays the Data

The React 19 frontend consumes this generated data through clean REST endpoints served by FastAPI:

1. **Dashboard & Fleet Surveillance (`/api/dashboard/{well_id}`)**:
   - Queries `sensor_readings` for latest hourly values.
   - Calculates real-time field aggregates across all 5 wells (Total BPD, Fleet Health, Active Alarms).
   - Shows well-specific KPIs (e.g. `BGW-02` shows $62.4^\circ\text{C}$ and $2,250\text{ cP}$; `BGW-03` shows $114.5^\circ\text{C}$ and $168.2\text{ BPD}$).
2. **Interactive 2D Wellbore Schematic (`/api/digital-twin/{well_id}`)**:
   - Uses reservoir temperature and pressure to position dynamic thermal plume radius, rod string tension colors, and pump fillage indicators.
3. **Historical Analytics (`/api/analytics/{well_id}?days=30`)**:
   - Queries `production_history` for 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day interactive area and line charts.
4. **Dynacard Diagnostics (`/api/srp/{well_id}/dynacards/latest`)**:
   - Displays 40-point surface and pump dynacard polygons. `BGW-02` renders severe friction hysteresis; `BGW-05` renders downstroke fluid pound notch.
5. **AI Predictions (`/api/prediction`)**:
   - Scikit-learn regressors trained directly on the 10,800 records predict forward production and thermal decay with $R^2 > 0.99$.
6. **Multi-Objective Optimization (`/api/optimization/run`)**:
   - Uses the latest well state from the database as baseline to solve Pareto-optimal SPM and steam volume trade-offs.
7. **Predictive Maintenance & Alarms (`/api/maintenance/{well_id}`, `/api/alerts`)**:
   - Surfaces active warnings (e.g., rod floating hazard on `BGW-02`, incomplete pump fillage on `BGW-05`).
